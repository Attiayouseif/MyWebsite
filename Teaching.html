Academic Pedagogy

Teaching Philosophy

"My teaching combines communicative language instruction with cultural competence and project-based learning, so students can engage Arabic as language, discourse, and lived experience."

Core Methodology

Pedagogical Framework

Dr. Youseif instructs Arabic language courses across all proficiency levels, from novice to superior. His graduate-level instruction includes the core Technology in the Arabic Language Classroom for the TAFL MA program, equipping future educators with digital fluency.

His curriculum design emphasizes advanced academic discourse, intercultural competence, and the practical application of linguistics in contemporary Arab issues, media, and professional communication.

Strategic Pillars

  • Project-Based Language Learning (PBLL) for advanced multiliteracy.
  • Integration of AI for immediate feedback and writing evaluation.
  • Authentic materials focus: Media, Cinema, and Contemporary Challenges.
  • Global Classroom collaboration (AUB/AUC) on minority representation.
