Nurturing Growth

Bridging the gap between Arabic Linguistics and innovative pedagogy.

Attia Youseif is a Senior Lecturer of Arabic at Indiana University. His work focuses on Arabic linguistics, Arabic language pedagogy, Arabic dialectology, Arabic cultural instruction, and technology-based learning materials for Arabic learners. He has taught Arabic across multiple proficiency levels and developed content-based Arabic courses on media, cinema, minorities in the Middle East, contemporary Arab issues, business communication, and Arabic language and culture.
